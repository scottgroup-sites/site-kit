# Care and health providers (site-kit)

These rules apply in addition to `CLAUDE.base.md` on sites for care and health providers.

- **Never present an unconfirmed claim as fact.** Statements about care, staffing, licensing, medical
  services, pricing, availability, hours, awards or outcomes are flagged `CONFIRM` (or `MADE UP` for
  stand-in details) until the provider confirms them.
- **Privacy:** no resident or patient names, photos, stories or health details without written
  consent from the person or their representative.
- **Testimonials** must be real and permitted. Never write one.
- **Forms never ask for medical information:** no fields for diagnoses, conditions, medications,
  insurance or ID numbers. The `sensitive-fields` check enforces this.
- **No promised outcomes,** and no services the provider isn't licensed to offer.
